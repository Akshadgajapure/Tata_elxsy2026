# Experiment Report: Multiple Streams + Low-Latency Gaming
**Scenario ID**: `streams_gaming` | **Date**: 2026-10-18 19:46:57 UTC

### Objective
Three adaptive video streams compete with a latency-sensitive gaming flow. Measures Jain's fairness index and gaming packet delay.

### Baseline vs. Adaptive QoS Comparison

| Metric | No QoS (FIFO) | Static QoS | Adaptive QoS (TataElexy) | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gaming Latency Ms** | 84.80 | 22.00 | 11.20 | ▼ 86.8% reduction |
| **Gaming Jitter Ms** | 18.20 | 3.40 | 1.10 | ▼ 94.0% reduction |
| **Stream Fairness Index** | 0.89 | 0.91 | 0.98 | ▲ +10.7% |
| **Total Throughput Mbps** | 24.80 | 21.00 | 24.60 | -0.8% |
| **Provenance** | 10-trial discrete queueing sample mean | 10-trial discrete queueing sample mean | 10-trial discrete queueing sample mean | — |

### Key Findings
Across 10 trials, Adaptive QoS achieved Jain's Fairness Index of 0.983 across streams while isolating gaming traffic to 11.2 ms latency (vs. 84.8 ms under No QoS).
