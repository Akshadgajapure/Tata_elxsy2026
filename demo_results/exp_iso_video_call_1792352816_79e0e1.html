ISO Download + Interactive Call

Scenario: iso_video_call | Executed: 2026-10-18 19:46:56 UTC

A large ISO download starts while an interactive video call is active. Evaluates whether QoS protects call latency and jitter from bufferbloat.

Key Finding: Across 10 workload trials, No QoS suffered severe bufferbloat (69.3 ms RTT). Adaptive QoS reduced call latency by 81.7% (to 12.7 ms) using FQ-CoDel queue isolation while enabling bulk traffic to borrow surplus bandwidth (42.5 Mbps), substantially outperforming Static QoS which rigid-capped bulk at 22.0 Mbps.
